TRUE NORTH: REDIRECTED
by Nneka Adewale-Mortimer

Captain Isbister's approach was exactly as organized as the intelligence file promised. Threading ran the outer subnet in two passes — the first to map, the second to move. The mapping pass was visible to the Syndicate's monitoring team and provided approximately ninety seconds of advance warning for each subsequent approach. The warning was used.

ICE was repositioned during the mapping pass. The navigation data archive's approach vectors were altered between Isbister's reconnaissance and her first actual run. Threading's route optimization mapped a path that no longer existed when she took it.

She adapted quickly. The second route was found within three minutes. This is the skill of a captain who thinks institutionally: improvisation within a plan rather than improvisation instead of a plan. The reroute was efficient and Isbister reached the archive's outer layer on the second run.

The outer layer held. The navigation data was not accessed.

The Syndicate scored seven points through the engagement, advancing navigation infrastructure agendas while Isbister's Threading rig worked on finding the revised approach vectors. Isbister scored two points from a remote server that the Syndicate's repositioning had left temporarily under-defended — a cost of the repositioning that the after-action report accepts as reasonable given the alternative.

True north for the Syndicate is the same direction it has always been.
