LUCKY STRIKE
by Orla Vaszquez-Nkosi

The name in the file is Barry "Baz" Wong. The handle suggests someone who has been running for a while — long enough to acquire a nickname, long enough for the nickname to appear in official Syndicate incident records. The deck is Lucky Bravado.

The intelligence note on Lucky Bravado is brief and characteristically understated: *high-variance play style. Baz accepts significant risk for high-reward outcomes. Credit-hungry. Threat spikes unpredictably.*

The Syndicate's credit analysts have a phrase for the kind of runner who uses luck as a strategic asset: "volatility operator." Volatility operators are uncomfortable to face not because they are always dangerous but because they are unpredictably dangerous — there is a version of this engagement where Baz does something that the probability models classify as extremely unlikely and it works anyway.

The Syndicate's fuel distribution center in Heinlein Lower Ring processes the logistical infrastructure for seventeen orbital platforms. It is not a facility that benefits from instability. The engines that push fuel through the distribution network require consistent, stable operation.

Baz introduces instability by nature.

Your task is to create a defensive position stable enough that even a lucky strike cannot dislodge it — to stack the facility with enough redundancy that probability in Baz's favor is not sufficient on its own to create a breach.

Luck runs out. Make sure the Syndicate's luck lasts longer.

—
TARGET: Barry "Baz" Wong — Lucky Bravado
FACILITY: Syndicate Fuel Distribution Center, Heinlein Lower Ring, Node 4
OBJECTIVE: Negate volatility advantage. Advance distribution infrastructure agendas. Deny high-risk server runs.
