NAILS
by Temidayo Okonkwo-Bell

Topan is not a runner in the traditional sense. The incident file classifies them as "organizer/activist" — someone who hacks not for personal profit but for the sake of a cause. That makes them, in the Syndicate's assessment, more dangerous than Zahya and more principled than Loup, which is a combination that keeps the counterintelligence team awake at night.

The Heinlein Bazaar District Server Farm is technically a subcontracted node — the Syndicate processes its logistics and retail data here, routing commercial traffic for the bazaar district's twelve hundred registered vendors. To a casual observer, it is completely mundane. To someone who knows what to look for, it is a waypoint in a supply chain that connects directly to Project Crow's material procurement network.

Topan's method — the one the runner community apparently calls "Prick Thyself" — involves deploying data fragments that mirror corporate architectures. Tools that let a runner understand how a network thinks, then turn that thinking against itself. Topan doesn't want to steal from the Syndicate. Topan wants to understand it.

Understanding is the precursor to exposure. Topan is building a file. A case. Whatever the cause is, the Syndicate is part of the story Topan is trying to tell.

Deny them the material. Score the logistics agendas and keep Project Crow's supply chain invisible.

—
TARGET: Topan — Prick Thyself
FACILITY: Heinlein Bazaar District Server Farm, Heinlein Commercial Zone
OBJECTIVE: Protect supply chain data. Score logistics agendas. Deny runner their investigative foothold.
