THE LIBERTADOR: UNAUTHORIZED
by Temidayo Okonkwo-Bell

Mercury collected three shibboleths. This is the honest assessment that begins the after-action report, without softening, because the threat posed by Mystic Shibboleth requires understanding the actual capability level it represents. Three shibboleths means three ICE configurations that Mercury can now re-enter at will — not in this engagement, but in any future engagement where those configurations appear.

The authorization core was not among the three.

The defensive strategy had prioritized the authorization core above all other servers — a resource allocation decision that left the outer and middle servers somewhat exposed, accepting the shibboleth collection risk on the less critical ICE to maintain the credit reserves needed for core protection. This is the kind of decision the after-action report calls "triage" and describes as correct.

The Syndicate scored seven agendas. Mercury scored three points — both through outer server access enabled by the early shibboleth acquisitions, and once through a middle server that the resource allocation had left under-defended.

The authorization core held. The identity verification infrastructure is intact. The shibboleths Mercury collected will require those three ICE configurations to be rotated before any future engagement, which the maintenance team has already flagged as a high-priority action.

The libertador found freedom in three rooms and a wall everywhere else.
