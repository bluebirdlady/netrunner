PRESSURE FRONT
by Orla Vaszquez-Nkosi

The Parhelion expansion arrives in the runner community like a solar flare — sudden, intensely bright, altering everything in its radius. The Syndicate's security division held an emergency briefing twelve hours after the first Parhelion-equipped runner was logged in the incident registry. The briefing lasted four hours. The summary document is forty-two pages.

You have read eleven of them.

MuslihaT is a familiar name in the registry. The Cezve Revolver configuration is new: a high-pressure run economy built around rapid cycling — credits generated, spent, generated again at a rate that outpaces conventional ICE economics. The Cezve Revolver doesn't wait for an opening. It creates one through sheer financial pressure.

The Syndicate's communications routing hub in the Heinlein orbital ring handles data traffic for the entire Lunar territory. In normal operations, the hub processes approximately twelve petabytes of traffic per hour. In a contested engagement, it becomes a strategic prize: whoever controls the routing controls what the Syndicate can communicate and to whom.

"MuslihaT has been retained by the coalition," the briefing officer says. "Not for profit this time. For strategic disruption."

The distinction matters. A runner working for profit calculates risk against reward and disengages when the math stops working. A runner working for strategic disruption has a different threshold for acceptable loss.

The pressure front is coming. Build the wall now.
