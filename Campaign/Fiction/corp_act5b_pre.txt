BELOW THE HORIZON
by Ivette Serrano-Park

The name in the file is Lat: Ethical Freelancer. The Syndicate's intelligence division has annotated the name with a small note: *self-styled. Do not interpret as a sincere value statement.* The annotation was added by someone with a dry sense of humor or someone who has worked runner cases long enough to find the irony necessary for self-preservation.

Lat's deck is called Lateral Thinking. That tells you something. A runner who names their tools is a runner who theorizes. They think in systems. They look for the move that does not occur to you until after it has already happened.

The Syndicate's research lab beneath the Heinlein transit hub is listed in internal documentation as a secondary facility, a designation that earns it roughly forty percent less ICE than a primary. Forty percent less ICE is exactly the kind of thing a lateral thinker looks for.

Your supervisor explains this to you in a tone that implies the vulnerability is somehow also your fault. You let this pass.

"Lat has a history of running simultaneous installations," the briefing continues. "Multiple tools going up at once, efficiency stacked, draw engines running underneath everything. If you let them set up, you will not be able to dislodge them."

You understand. The answer is speed. Advance before they are ready. Score before the engine fires.

Below the horizon, in the transit hub's sub-level, a research lab hums with data the Syndicate does not want read.

Do not let it be read.

—
TARGET: Lat: Ethical Freelancer — Lateral Thinking
FACILITY: Syndicate Research Lab, Heinlein Transit Hub, Sublevel 4
OBJECTIVE: Score agendas before runner engine establishes. Deny lateral access. Protect research data.
