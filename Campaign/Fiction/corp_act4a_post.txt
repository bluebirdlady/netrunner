DRONE SILENCE
by Ivette Serrano-Park

Magdalene's drones entered the outer subnet at 0200 — not the runner herself, but her automated forward scouts, probing ICE configurations and transmitting data back to wherever Magdalene was sitting. The monitoring team could see them, these little programs, moving through the outer layer like insects across glass.

The ICE was ready for them. Not the standard configuration. Something prepared specifically for automated forward-scout packages, designed by the Syndicate's defensive architecture team over the previous seventy-two hours using behavioral models reconstructed from Magdalene's Floor 99 engagement.

The first wave of drones met walls they couldn't classify. They transmitted back data about the walls. Magdalene processed it. She sent a second wave, adapted.

The second wave met different walls.

Magdalene herself entered at 0318 and ran against an architecture that had been changing in response to her own reconnaissance. The coordination node agenda scored at 0401, while she was still building her model of a network that would not hold still.

She reached the second server at 0445. The coordination node's emergency partition protocol sealed at 0446.

At 0503, Magdalene disconnected.

—
The drones mapped a maze that kept rebuilding itself.
Every wall they reported was already moving.
Black Site Alpha holds.
