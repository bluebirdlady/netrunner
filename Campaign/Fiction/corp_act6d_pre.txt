THE LEARNING CURVE
by Desh Fuentes-Calloway

The runner in the file goes by Dewi. Single name, no handle, no affiliation listed beyond the designation Groovy — which the intelligence division's annotation describes as "a deck philosophy centered on resource accumulation and adaptive play." The analyst who wrote the annotation seems faintly puzzled by the name. The Syndicate does not have much cultural vocabulary for Groovy.

Dewi is not experienced. That is the assessment. The deck shows promise but the play patterns are inconsistent — moments of genuine insight interrupted by decisions that a more seasoned runner would not make. A learner.

This should, in theory, be a simpler engagement.

Your supervisor's counterpoint: "Learners are unpredictable. They don't play the optimal line because they don't always know what it is. You build ICE for the expected run path and they take the unexpected one by accident and suddenly you've got a problem."

The Syndicate's training data center in the Lunacent sublevel system handles knowledge management for the entire Heinlein territory. The irony of a learning runner attacking a learning facility is not lost on you. You let it pass, because irony is not protective.

Groovy is building. Dewi is figuring out the system. The system the Syndicate runs is not a friendly one.

Teach them that lesson before they teach themselves a better one.

—
TARGET: Dewi — Groovy
FACILITY: Syndicate Training Data Center, Lunacent Sublevel 11
OBJECTIVE: Prevent runner from establishing resource base. Advance knowledge management agendas. Deny adaptive access.
