BURN NOTICE
by Nneka Adewale-Mortimer

Ryo is back, and the deck has changed again. Fizzy Phoenix has been replaced by something with a different energy entirely: Classy Fire. The intelligence division's annotation reads: *escalated. Previous rig focused on efficiency; Classy Fire focuses on controlled destruction. Ryo has been radicalized toward the coalition's harder-line positions.*

The shift is significant. A runner who changes their tools changes their philosophy. Ryo's orange flag in the incident registry has been annotated: *monitor for further escalation.*

Classy Fire burns selectively — targeted asset destruction paired with surgical access, a combination that the previous Ryo configuration did not carry. It is still elegant. Elegance is the constant. But the fire is real now.

The Syndicate's executive entertainment facility in Lunacent's upper tower district is a peculiar target. It hosts the quarterly meetings where the Syndicate's subsidiary directors present to the main board — a venue for the kind of conversation that never appears in any formal record because it happens between courses and over drinks in secure rooms. What happens in those rooms is the Syndicate's most sensitive information.

A burn notice is a termination of relationship. Someone has decided Ryo is done being cautious.

You have been asked to decide the same about the facility.

Classy Fire does not forgive openings. Do not create one.

—
TARGET: Ryo Phoenix Ono — Classy Fire
FACILITY: Syndicate Executive Entertainment Facility, Lunacent Upper Tower District, Suite 7
OBJECTIVE: Protect executive meeting records. Deny selective access. Advance executive infrastructure agendas.
