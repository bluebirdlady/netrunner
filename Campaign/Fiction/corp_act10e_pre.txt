OPEN REVOLT
by Desh Fuentes-Calloway

The intelligence briefing for this engagement does not use the word revolt because the intelligence briefing has been written by people who use precise language, and the precise language they have chosen is: *coordinated coalition assault entering primary phase.*

This is the primary phase.

Esa Afontov's red flag was added to the incident registry forty-eight hours ago. There are now three red flags in the active roster. The registry has never carried three simultaneous red flags. The Syndicate's counterintelligence team added a note below the third flag: *the coalition's field operators are committed and coordinated at a level equivalent to a state-level actor.*

Face is the configuration. A single word. No metaphor, no elaboration. The intelligence division's cultural annotation: *direct. Esa Afontov has stopped naming decks and started stating position.*

The Syndicate's executive command center in the Lunacent secure district is the operational heart of the Syndicate's territorial government. Not the public-facing chambers — those are ceremonial. The command center is the room where decisions are made, where the Executive Director gives direction, where the Syndicate's response to everything from regulatory challenges to runner coalition escalation is formulated and executed.

The runners are at the door of the room where the Syndicate decides what to do about runners.

Open revolt. They have chosen the most pointed target possible.

This is the moment. This is what everything before was building toward.

—
TARGET: Esa Afontov — Face
FACILITY: Syndicate Executive Command Center, Lunacent Secure District, Decision Core
OBJECTIVE: Defend the command center against primary-phase coalition assault. Advance executive authority agendas. Hold the decision core.
