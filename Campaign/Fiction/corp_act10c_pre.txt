RECKONING
by Temidayo Okonkwo-Bell

Vic is back, and the Courage configuration replaces Red Act. This is not a softening — the intelligence division's note is explicit. Courage is Red Act with everything that limited Red Act removed. The commitment is the same. The ideology is the same. The capability is significantly greater.

The briefing officer uses the word reckoning without being prompted, and then appears to notice the word and decides not to retrieve it.

The runner community's public communications — the channels the Syndicate monitors through its own signal analysis infrastructure — have been using the word openly for the past ten days. Reckoning. The runners believe they are approaching a moment of account. The Syndicate's intelligence division has flagged this language as indicating coordinated mobilization.

Courage is built for this moment. A principled runner with nothing to prove and everything to do.

The Syndicate's asset management center in the Lunacent investment district holds the credit reserves, investment portfolios, and asset registers that fund the entire Syndicate operational budget. The Syndicate runs on money. Every facility, every ICE subroutine, every maintenance cycle is paid from the reserves that the asset management center holds.

A runner targeting the asset management center is targeting the Syndicate's ability to function.

This is the reckoning Vic believes in. Do not give it to them.

—
TARGET: Vic — Courage
FACILITY: Syndicate Asset Management Center, Lunacent Investment District, Reserve Core
OBJECTIVE: Protect credit reserves and asset registers. Deny access to investment infrastructure. Advance asset management agendas.
