SHADOW PROTOCOL: ENDURED
by Ivette Serrano-Park

Nosferatu ran for twenty-two turns. The Syndicate's record for longest single engagement in the current campaign was eleven. Hiram 0mission ran for twenty-two turns and the attrition was real and sustained throughout — each turn extracting some fraction of the Syndicate's credit reserve, defensive asset, or operational capacity. Slow, methodical, deliberate.

The counterintelligence center held.

There was a moment at turn seventeen when the Syndicate's credit reserve dropped below the threshold the monitoring team had designated as critical — the level at which ICE maintenance could no longer be guaranteed without emergency reallocation. Emergency reallocation occurred. Assets were consumed. Two non-critical servers were deliberately left without outer ICE to maintain the inner defensive layer's integrity.

Hiram scored through those two servers. Two points.

The Syndicate scored seven agendas, the last two in a compressed window between turn nineteen and twenty, when the attrition had depleted Nosferatu's own economy to the point where the rate of drain slowed enough to create an advancement gap.

Twenty-two turns. Seven to two. The counterintelligence infrastructure is intact.

The after-action report's final line: *this was a probe. The intelligence division's pre-engagement assessment has been confirmed. Coordinated coalition assault is imminent.*

The shadow has passed. The event it preceded is coming.

—
Twenty-two turns of attrition. The center did not fall.
Hiram tested endurance. The Syndicate has endurance.
The shadow is noted. The event that follows is expected.
