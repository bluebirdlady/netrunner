PROJECT CROW: COMPLETE
by Orla Vaszquez-Nkosi

MuslihaT arrived at the Executive Suite with a disinformation payload designed by a firm that does not officially exist, funded by a coalition whose membership the Syndicate's counterintelligence team has spent four months partially reconstructing, orchestrated by a man who once worked for Haas-Bioroid and found, on the other side of a settlement, a reason to unmake what he had helped to build.

They found walls they had not expected.

MuslihaT is a pragmatist. When the disinformation payload hit the Executive Suite's ICE and the ICE did not fall, and when the credit reserve began to drain faster than the engagement's budget model had projected, MuslihaT began the calculation that any pragmatist begins: is this profitable.

The answer the calculation produced was no.

They tried three servers. They scored nothing. They withdrew at 1312.

The final Syndicate agenda resolved at 1247. Project Crow's completion documentation transferred to the Lunar Commerce Authority's sealed registry at 1300 — registered as an infrastructure development initiative, compliant with all applicable regulations, presented with four years of supporting documentation, a legal team's masterwork, pristine.

The Executive Director's voice, when it comes through the speaker: "Well done."

The dome outside is silver with Earthshine. The transplas catches the light the way it always does — cold and beautiful and indifferent to everything that has happened beneath it.

—
Project Crow is complete.
Everything protected. Everything that needed to happen, happened.
The Syndicate endures. This is what profit over principle looks like from the inside.
