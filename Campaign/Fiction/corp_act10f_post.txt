PROFIT OVER PRINCIPLE: SEALED
by Ivette Serrano-Park

The after-action report for this engagement has no standard format. There is no monitoring team summary, no credit reserve analysis, no maintenance assessment. What exists is a single document, written by a single person, from memory, after the communication systems were restored following the engagement's conclusion.

Trojan.Exe did not arrive with elegance. It arrived with everything the coalition had.

The Executive Suite's outer defensive layer fell in the first three minutes. Not damaged — fallen. The ICE that remained was the inner layer, the private server layer, the Project Crow core. The suites around the agenda servers were exposed. The credit reserve was burning to maintain what could be maintained.

Arissana Rocha Nahu ran the suite eight times. Eight runs. Five of them reached the innermost ICE. Two of those five broke through the innermost ICE and found the second-innermost ICE, which had not appeared on any threat model Trojan.Exe had built because the second-innermost ICE did not exist in any previous engagement — the Syndicate had installed it in the forty-eight hours before this engagement based on intelligence the coalition did not know the Syndicate possessed.

Intelligence is what the Syndicate does. It is what the Syndicate has always done.

Arissana scored six points. The engagement nearly failed three times in the window between turn nine and turn twelve. The documents will show, when they are eventually reviewed by whatever body reviews them, that the Syndicate's seventh agenda point was scored at turn twelve while the Project Crow completion documentation was simultaneously transmitting to the Lunar Commerce Authority's sealed registry.

The transmission completed.

Project Crow is sealed.

Arissana Rocha Nahu disconnected at turn thirteen. The Syndicate's monitoring systems, restored at turn fifteen, showed her disconnecting into the Lunar network. She is out there. The coalition is out there. The revolt that began with forty-seven new runner signatures and ends here — or doesn't end here, because revolts do not end, they transform — has produced something the Syndicate's analysts will spend years characterizing.

The Executive Suite's dome still catches Earthshine. Cold and beautiful and indifferent.

The Syndicate endures.

Project Crow is complete. Everything protected. Everything that needed to happen, happened. The Syndicate is still here.

—
Project Crow is complete. The registry is sealed. The Syndicate survived.
Everything the coalition built, everything it learned, everything it sent —
it was not enough. This time. The dome catches Earthshine. The Syndicate endures.
