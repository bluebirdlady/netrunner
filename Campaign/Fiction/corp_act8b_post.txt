ACT OF WILL: BLOCKED
by Desh Fuentes-Calloway

Vic ran the legal processing center with the sustained pressure the threat profile had predicted. Red Act did not disengage when the first ICE subroutine held. Red Act did not disengage when the credit reserve dropped below the break-even threshold for the outer server. Red Act kept running, each failed attempt adding to a resource cost that a profit-motivated runner would have classified as prohibitive several turns earlier.

The legal archive did not fall.

The ICE on the legal processing center's core server had been stacked with this specific threat in mind: a runner who will not stop. Deep layers, expensive to beat, requiring resources that sustained engagement eventually exhausts even for a committed ideologue. The Syndicate's credit engine ran efficiently throughout, continuously refreshing the defensive infrastructure while Vic's repeated runs gradually depleted the Red Act economy.

Vic scored four points. This is the highest single-engagement runner score the Syndicate has recorded this quarter. Four points came through the outer servers — not the legal archive, not the core. The regulatory filing agendas in the core advanced cleanly.

Final score: seven to four. The legal archive was not accessed. The contract adjudication infrastructure is intact.

The after-action report notes: *Red Act is a sustained threat. Four points is not a comfortable margin. Re-evaluate core ICE depth for future engagements.*

The Syndicate notes and re-evaluates.

—
The act of will met the wall of will.
The legal archive holds. Regulatory filings registered.
Seven to four. The law, for now, is the Syndicate's.
