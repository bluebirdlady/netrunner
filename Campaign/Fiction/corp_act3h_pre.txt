CORPORATE RAIDERS
by Temidayo Okonkwo-Bell

Dewi Subrotoputri describes herself, in the runner community's public forums, as an enthusiast. The word is not ironic. She approaches unauthorized network access with the energy of a graduate student who has found a genuinely interesting problem and cannot stop turning it over in their hands.

The incident file notes: "High technical creativity. Significant pattern recognition capability. Academic background in network topology and distributed systems." The psychological note at the bottom adds: "Motivated primarily by intellectual curiosity rather than political ideology or financial gain. This may make her more dangerous, not less."

Dewi is not interested in stealing from the Syndicate R&D Campus. She is interested in understanding it.

The R&D Campus houses the Syndicate's active research pipeline — thirty-one ongoing projects at various stages of development, ranging from routine pharmaceutical refinement to the hardware fabrication work that feeds directly into Project Crow. Dewi's published research papers (the counterintelligence team has reviewed them all) demonstrate a particular fascination with what she calls "emergent network architectures" — systems that evolve beyond their original design parameters.

Project Crow is exactly that kind of system.

If Dewi reaches the Campus's inner research nodes, she won't just find data to steal. She'll understand what the Syndicate is building. And she will publish her findings.

—
TARGET: Dewi Subrotoputri — Enthusiasm
FACILITY: Syndicate R&D Campus, Lunacent Research Ring
OBJECTIVE: Protect active research pipeline. Score R&D agendas. Prevent researcher from mapping Project Crow architecture.
