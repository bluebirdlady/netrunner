SHADOW PROTOCOL
by Ivette Serrano-Park

The Rebellion without Rehearsal expansion is not a capability upgrade. The Syndicate's intelligence division has been clear about this in the restricted briefings: this is a declaration. The runners who have been, until now, operating in something like a grey area — technically criminal, philosophically contested, tolerated by a Commerce Authority that finds regulatory ambiguity convenient — are no longer in that grey area.

They are in revolt.

Hiram 0mission's Nosferatu configuration is the first direct engagement the Syndicate has recorded from the coalition's strategic architect since the ghost protocol incident at the biomedical auxiliary servers. That engagement stayed off the main registry. This one cannot.

Nosferatu is built around sustained attrition — a slow drain, resource by resource, that weakens the Syndicate's defensive infrastructure not through single decisive strikes but through relentless incremental reduction. The name is not coincidental. Something that drains its target over time, leaving it diminished and unable to respond at critical moments.

The shadow protocol designation comes from the Syndicate's counterintelligence team, who have flagged this engagement as the precursor operation to something larger — a probe in advance of a coordinated multi-runner assault that the intelligence division has been anticipating for six weeks.

This is not the main event. This is the shadow before the event.

Hiram 0mission is testing the Syndicate's endurance.

Show him what endurance looks like.
