CULTIVATION: INTERRUPTED
by Temidayo Okonkwo-Bell

Magdalene Keino-Chemutai ran the agricultural station in a way that the monitoring team initially struggled to classify as a threat. The first four turns were quiet — resources accumulating, Fermented Future's engine assembling itself in the background while the visible activity remained minimal. A runner who looks passive until they aren't.

The Syndicate did not wait for Magdalene to stop looking passive.

The pressure point was identified at turn three: a credit expenditure event that the patience engine required to move from the accumulation phase to the operational phase. The Syndicate's response was to force the credit drain before the operational phase could begin — aggressive ICE rezzing, assets deployed specifically to tax the runner's economy rather than protect the inner servers, a configuration built around disruption rather than defense.

It worked. The credit drain hit the patience engine at turn five, forcing Magdalene to delay the transition to operational mode. The delay cost four turns. In those four turns, the Syndicate advanced five agendas.

Magdalene did eventually reach operational phase. Fermented Future, once running, is difficult to stop. She scored three points in the final three turns of the engagement. The Syndicate had seven before the operational phase began.

Final score: seven to three. The food science contracts are registered.

Cultivation interrupted at the root.
Patience met aggression. Aggression set the pace.
Fermented Future arrived too late. The contracts are ours.
