PROFIT OVER PRINCIPLE: FINALE
by Ivette Serrano-Park

There is no new briefing. There is no voice through a speaker. There is no supervisor, no packet, no encrypted document. The Executive Director's office has gone dark on all normal communication channels. What arrives instead is a single text string through the Syndicate's most protected internal relay:

*Arissana Rocha Nahu. Trojan.Exe. Executive Suite. Now.*

You already know what this is. The Syndicate's intelligence division has been modeling this scenario for four months. The final coordinated assault. The runner the coalition has been holding — the one who doesn't appear on red flag registries because the Syndicate didn't know she was the finisher until now. Arissana Rocha Nahu. The Lilypad Gamble's evolution: Trojan.Exe.

Trojan.Exe is the runner community's most advanced known configuration. The intelligence division's briefing note, written in advance of an engagement they did not know the timing for, reads: *everything. Trojan.Exe uses everything. Built from four years of runner coalition shared intelligence, operational data from every engagement in the campaign, a configuration that represents the sum total of what the coalition has learned about the Syndicate's defensive capabilities.*

They built a tool using what they learned from every time we stopped them.

The Executive Suite. Floor one hundred and two. Earthshine through the dome. Project Crow, eighteen months from completion, sealed in the Syndicate's deepest servers.

This is the last defense. The only defense.

Everything the Syndicate has. Everything that matters.

—
TARGET: Arissana Rocha Nahu — Trojan.Exe
FACILITY: Syndicate Executive Suite, Floor 102, Lunacent
OBJECTIVE: Score all remaining agenda points. Complete Project Crow. Defend the Executive Suite. Survive.
