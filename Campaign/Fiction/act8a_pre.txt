EXTRACT. EXPORT. EXCEL.
by Circuito Saudade

The manifest was not secret. That was the first thing the analyst noticed.

Ob Superheavy Logistics maintained a public ledger of orbital cargo movements — a regulatory requirement under the new Automata Initiative compliance framework, which mandated transparency in supply chains involving any bioroid-adjacent labour. The ledger was updated every six hours, cross-referenced with Heinlein Customs, and freely accessible to any licensed auditor.

What the ledger did not show was what the cargo actually was.

The declared contents of shipment OBX-7714-VEGA: "reclaimed construction materials, classification 4C, non-hazardous." Weight: 312 metric tons. Origin: Ob extraction facility, Heinlein polar crater array. Destination: Weyland Consortium staging depot, Earth transfer orbit.

The analyst had cross-referenced OBX-7714-VEGA with seventeen other manifests across six months. The weight figures for "reclaimed construction materials, classification 4C" fluctuated by plus or minus two percent across all seventeen shipments — a variance small enough to read as normal cargo settling, large enough to suggest something was being carefully subtracted from each load before weighting.

312 metric tons of material, seventeen times, at a variance of six metric tons per shipment.

102 metric tons unaccounted for across six months. Moving in small parcels, in plain sight, through a public ledger.

She had sent the analysis to her supervisor. Her supervisor had thanked her for her diligence and told her the variance was within acceptable limits.

Her supervisor had a Weyland Consortium badge on her desk that had never been there before.

The analyst had found, through a chain of contacts she did not entirely trust, a runner designation. She had forwarded the analysis to that designation at 3 a.m. and not slept since.

—

TARGET: Ob Superheavy Logistics: Extract. Export. Excel.
FACILITY: Ob Orbital Operations Hub, Heinlein Equatorial Transfer Station
OBJECTIVE: Pull the cargo metadata. Find where the subtracted tonnage is going and why Weyland needs it hidden in a compliant ledger.
