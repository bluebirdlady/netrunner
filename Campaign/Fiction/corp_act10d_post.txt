WEARABLE TECH: INTACT
by Orla Vaszquez-Nkosi

The integrated configuration performed as the threat profile predicted — better than Trickster Paladin alone, better than Midnight Wolf alone, better than either plus the coordination layer that Midnight Wolf had operated. The combination of social engineering, real-time coalition analysis, and Rebellion without Rehearsal augmentation creates an approach capability that the Syndicate's countermeasure team has classified as "current-ceiling threat."

Current-ceiling. The top of what the coalition has demonstrated in the field.

The hardware development center held against the current ceiling.

The component core's ICE had been rebuilt following the after-action recommendations from three previous engagements with Loup. Not incrementally updated — rebuilt, with a configuration philosophy that does not assume a single threat vector because Loup's integrated approach attacks all threat vectors simultaneously. The rebuilt ICE held through all three layers of the integrated attack.

Loup scored four points. Through social engineering access on outer server one — a gap the Syndicate had accepted because closing it would have revealed the new core configuration's philosophy to the coordination layer. One agenda conceded as camouflage. Three points through aggressive mid-engagement pressure when the coordination layer's analysis correctly identified a credit gap.

The hardware specifications are intact. The component architecture is intact.

The chisel found purchase on the surface. The structure beneath is unchanged.
