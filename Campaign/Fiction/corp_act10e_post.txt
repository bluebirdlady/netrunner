OPEN REVOLT: TURNED
by Desh Fuentes-Calloway

Face arrived without decoration. No misdirection, no coordination games, no patience engine, no probability calculations — Esa Afontov ran the executive command center with the Tungsten Carnivore's destructive capacity directed entirely at a single target, augmented with Rebellion without Rehearsal tools that the Syndicate's countermeasure library had not fully characterized before the engagement began.

Three ICE subroutines were destroyed in the first four turns. The executive command center's outer defensive layer no longer exists. The after-action maintenance assessment lists it as requiring complete reconstruction.

The decision core held.

The Syndicate's defensive response was not elegant. It was not strategic. It was the use of every available credit, every available asset, every available countermeasure, deployed at maximum intensity against a single objective: keep the decision core protected until seven agenda points could be scored.

The seventh agenda point was scored at turn thirteen. Esa Afontov reached the second layer of the decision core at turn fourteen. Turn fourteen was one turn too late.

The Syndicate won by one turn.

Final score: seven to six. Esa scored six points through the destroyed outer defensive layer. Six points is the highest single-engagement runner score in the current campaign. The second-highest was five, recorded twice.

The command center made its decision. Esa Afontov could not stop it.

One turn. The revolt was turned by one turn.

—
Six points. Seven points. One turn separated revolt from victory.
The command center's outer layer is gone. The core gave the orders it needed to give.
Open revolt turned back. The Syndicate decides. It will continue to decide.
