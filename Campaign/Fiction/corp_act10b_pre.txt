HOWLING
by Nneka Adewale-Mortimer

The pack is moving.

René "Loup" Arcemont's designation in the incident registry has been upgraded to red flag — second red flag in the active roster, alongside Hiram 0mission. The intelligence division's annotation: *confirmed coalition inner circle. Operational planning, field execution, cross-runner coordination.* The wolf is not running alone. The wolf is running for the pack.

Midnight Wolf is a new configuration — not an update to Trickster Paladin but a replacement. The social engineering elements remain, but Midnight Wolf adds a coordination layer: tools that sync with other active runner operations, that share real-time data on Syndicate defensive responses, that feed information to the coalition's analysis network while the engagement is occurring.

This is not a runner. This is a node in a network.

The Syndicate's operational scheduling center in Heinlein Station coordinates the timing of every major Syndicate facility operation across the territorial claim — maintenance windows, agenda advancement schedules, the moments when facilities are at their most and least defended. If Midnight Wolf gets into the scheduling center and begins transmitting to the coalition's analysis network, the Syndicate's operational rhythm becomes the coalition's intelligence asset.

The howling is the signal the pack is converging. Silence it.

—
TARGET: René "Loup" Arcemont — Midnight Wolf
FACILITY: Syndicate Operational Scheduling Center, Heinlein Station, Coordination Core
OBJECTIVE: Deny scheduling data access. Prevent real-time coalition data transmission. Protect operational rhythm. Advance scheduling infrastructure agendas.
