COMPLIANCE: VERIFIED
by Ivette Serrano-Park

Zahya's credentials passed the first three validation layers without triggering a single alarm. The runner had invested heavily — the spoofing suite was the best the Syndicate's analysts had seen outside of state-sponsored intelligence operations. Zahya was inside PT Untaian's public-facing network before the monitoring team registered the access as anomalous.

But the inner compliance layer had been restructured at 0200. The architecture behind PT Untaian's legitimate front-end no longer connected to the research database in any path that Zahya's credential tree could predict. The biomedical research existed in a subnet that appeared, from Zahya's position, to be an archival storage node for historical clinical trial data.

The runner spent three hours in PT Untaian's network. They found nine years of historical clinical trial documentation and a drug interaction database that is, in fact, publicly available from the Lunar Health Authority for a nominal licensing fee.

The biomedical research agenda scored at 0418. The second at 0502.

Zahya left a clean exit — no evidence of intrusion in the public-facing logs. Professional. The counterintelligence team respects that, even as they flag the credential profile for investigation.

—
Zahya had the right ticket.
The destination had changed.
The research travels on, unseized, in a direction they cannot follow.
