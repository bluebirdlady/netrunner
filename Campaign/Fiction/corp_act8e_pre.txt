DEEP CARTOGRAPHY
by Temidayo Okonkwo-Bell

Captain Padma Isbister has updated her configuration. Threading has been replaced by Dr. Nuka — a name the intelligence division traces to an independent cartography system developed outside corporate infrastructure. The implication is deliberate: Isbister has moved from efficient routing to comprehensive mapping. She is no longer threading through the shortest path. She is drawing the entire map.

Dr. Nuka is a deep access deck. It does not rush. It systematically identifies the structure of a facility's server architecture — every server, every ICE layer, every buried asset — and uses that comprehensive map to identify the approach that the conventional threat model would classify as impossible. The map, once drawn, reveals the route that should not exist.

This is a different problem than Threading.

The Syndicate's deep research archive in the Heinlein sub-orbital station is one of the most protected facilities in the Syndicate's territorial infrastructure. The archive holds twelve years of biomedical, materials, and engineering research — the intellectual foundation of Project Crow and every other major Syndicate initiative. It is protected with the understanding that conventional runners should not be able to reach the core.

Captain Isbister is drawing a map that may reveal a different understanding.

Deep cartography finds what should not be findable. The Syndicate needs every layer of that archive to be exactly what it appears to be: impenetrable.

—
TARGET: Captain Padma Isbister — Dr. Nuka
FACILITY: Syndicate Deep Research Archive, Heinlein Sub-Orbital Station, Core Level
OBJECTIVE: Deny comprehensive mapping of archive structure. Protect research core. Advance deep research agendas.
