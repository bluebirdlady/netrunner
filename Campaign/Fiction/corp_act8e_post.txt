DEEP CARTOGRAPHY: OBSCURED
by Temidayo Okonkwo-Bell

Captain Isbister drew the map. This is the honest assessment: by turn eight, Dr. Nuka had produced a structural model of the deep research archive that the Syndicate's counterintelligence team has classified as approximately seventy percent accurate. The remaining thirty percent consists of the archive's most sensitive layers — the ones that were deliberately structured to appear as archive continuation rather than as a distinct research core.

Seventy percent is a significant map. It is not a complete one.

The run on the core came at turn nine, using the seventy percent map to identify an approach the conventional threat model would have rated as unlikely. The approach was correct for seventy percent of the archive. It encountered the obscured thirty percent and stopped.

The deep research archive held at its core.

The Syndicate scored seven agendas through the engagement, advancing them in the windows between Isbister's mapping passes — a schedule that the monitoring team had to maintain with significant discipline, watching for the mapping completion signals and accelerating agenda advancement when a window appeared.

Isbister scored two points. Both came from outer research servers that were not part of the sensitive core — a concession the Syndicate's defensive model had anticipated.

The map that exists is seventy percent accurate. It is missing the most important thirty percent.

The archive holds what it must hold.

—
The map was drawn. The core was not on it.
Seventy percent is not enough to reach the center.
Deep research secure. The archive holds its most important secrets.
