RECKONING: DEFERRED
by Temidayo Okonkwo-Bell

Courage is exactly what the name describes. The engagement record shows Vic running the asset management center's reserve core seven times — seven runs, the first six of which ended at the ICE layer, the seventh of which ended at the second ICE layer. Seven attempts. Each one without visible hesitation between failure and re-attempt.

Principled runners do not hesitate. This is the hardest kind to defend against, because the calculation that causes other runners to withdraw — the point at which the probability no longer favors the attempt — does not apply to Courage. Vic is not asking whether this is profitable. Vic is asking whether this is possible.

The answer, for all seven runs, was: not today.

The reserve core held. The credit reserves are intact. The investment portfolios were not accessed. The asset registers were not modified.

Vic scored five points. The highest score in any single engagement in the current campaign, matching the previous record held by Esa Afontov. Five points came through outer servers where the ICE had been stripped by the sustained pressure of seven core runs — the credit cost of defending the reserve core left the peripheral infrastructure exposed and Vic used the exposure.

Seven to five. The narrowest margin in the current campaign.

The reckoning that Vic believes in has been deferred, not denied. The Syndicate knows the difference.

—
Seven runs at the reserve core. The seventh found the second layer.
Reckoning deferred. Five points, seven points. The margin is real.
The reserves hold. The Syndicate can still afford to fight.
