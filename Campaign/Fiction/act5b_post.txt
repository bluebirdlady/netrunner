The unpublished research notes are not written in the language of science.

They're written in the language of people who knew what they were doing and had made their peace with it. Specific. Unsentimental. A record of decisions made efficiently by people who had been told the decisions had already been made elsewhere, and who had therefore chosen not to think about them.

---

The correspondence between the Méliès U lead researcher and the PT Untaian project coordinator spans twenty-two months. It is mostly logistical — substrate delivery schedules, expression confirmation timelines, notes on refining the durability protocol language for the final papers, because some of the original phrasing was "too transparent about mechanism" and needed to be made "appropriately technical."

The final message in the chain is from the Méliès U researcher. It contains three sentences.

The first sentence asks whether the researchers involved will have exposure once the project reaches deployment phase.

The second sentence says they understand if the answer can't be put in writing.

The third sentence says they will proceed regardless.

There is no response on record.

Betwixt's warning was accurate. The Méliès U network has Jinteki-standard internal security, and there's a layer beneath the research archive that you don't touch — something that responded to your presence before you got close to it, in the way a security system does when it knows you're there and is deciding. You pull back. You already have what you came for.

You're four hops clear before their incident response triggers.

The picture is complete.

Financial records. Research data. Production manifests. Suppression orders with an authorization chain. And now: forty-seven paper drafts, the internal review comments on each, and twenty-two months of correspondence showing the gap between what was written and what was done.

There is one thing left.

H-982 is being demolished. BANGUN received a clearance contract six days ago. When the facility is gone, the corps will say that what you have is speculation — document fragments, data that could have come from anywhere. You need what's inside H-982's isolated archive before BANGUN finishes its work.

The physical record is the last piece. Without it, there is no specific place. Without a specific place, there is only a story.

You need to make it real.
