LIVING ARCHIVE
by Orla Vaszquez-Nkosi

Dewi is back. The runner who was learning has learned. The deck called Groovy is gone; the deck called Self-Modifying Pinhole has replaced it. The name is the philosophy: a pinhole that modifies itself, that adapts to whatever barrier it encounters, that is never the same tool twice because it reconfigures between applications.

The intelligence note on the updated Dewi is marked with a notation the Syndicate's analysts add when they have to revise a previous threat assessment upward: *significant capability increase from initial engagement. Prior categorization as developing-threat-tier is rescinded.*

Dewi is no longer developing. Dewi has developed.

The Syndicate's historical records facility in the Lunacent heritage district holds the corporate archive: founding documentation, territorial claim filings, the original licensing agreements with the Lunar Commerce Authority. The archive is called a living archive because it is continuously updated — new filings, new claims, new documentation of the Syndicate's ongoing legitimacy. It is the paper trail that makes everything else legal.

A self-modifying pinhole in a living archive is a specific kind of danger. The pinhole changes to fit whatever it needs to enter. The living archive contains the records that cannot be altered or they stop being legitimate.

Dewi has grown into a real threat. The living archive is irreplaceable.

Do not let the pinhole find a shape that fits.

—
TARGET: Dewi — Self-Modifying Pinhole
FACILITY: Syndicate Historical Records Facility, Lunacent Heritage District, Archive Core
OBJECTIVE: Protect living archive integrity. Deny self-modifying access to founding documentation. Advance archive maintenance agendas.
