AFTERMATH: COMMS CENTER
by Orla Vaszquez-Nkosi

Ryo hit the outer ICE at full speed and it cost them eight credits and a program. They hit it again. Seven credits. Another program. The logs are a sequence of collision events, each one slightly more depleted than the last.

The executive communications layer never came within access range. The scoring window opened at the fourth ICE layer, well behind the breach point Ryo managed to reach, and the agenda transferred clean.

By the time Ryo found a seam in the second server's approach, their rig was operating at reduced capacity. The anger was still present in the access pattern — that frenetic, non-economical push — but anger without resources is just momentum, and momentum runs out.

Disconnect at 1547. Incomplete run on server three.

The Comms Center's security coordinator files a damage report: fourteen ICE subroutines requiring repair or replacement, two asset nodes damaged in proximity to the runner's access path. Total cost: significant.

Total cost to the runner: more.
