DOWNFALL CASCADE: CONTAINED
by Desh Fuentes-Calloway

McCaffrey hit the server farm at 0840 orbital time with a rig that the Syndicate's threat library had never seen operating at full capacity. The Blueberry Breakers configuration allows hardware to be repurposed on the fly — ICE that would stop a standard runner bounced off the reconfigured assembly like it wasn't there.

What McCaffrey hadn't accounted for was depth.

The routing infrastructure archives have seven layers. Runners who probe hardware specialists tend to build for speed and flexibility. They do not always build for the long game. When the Syndicate's credit engine came online in the third engagement window and the ICE on servers three and four refreshed behind McCaffrey's position, the calculus changed.

McCaffrey scored two points before the outer subnet collapsed around them. The Syndicate scored seven. The legacy contracts resolved at 1103, routed cleanly through the Heinlein Commerce Node, and were registered before the runner coalition's monitoring systems could flag the transfer.

The server farm is intact. The routing infrastructure is intact. McCaffrey is somewhere in the high orbital ring, reconfiguring.

They will be back. That is the nature of this new phase. They are always back.

The Syndicate notes the threat and updates the matrix. Forty-eight new signatures now. Forty-eight.

—
The cascade reached us. We were ready.
Every server held. Every agenda landed.
The high orbital ring is ours, for now.
