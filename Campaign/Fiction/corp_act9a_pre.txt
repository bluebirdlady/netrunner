MACHINE POLITICS
by Desh Fuentes-Calloway

The Automata Initiative is not a runner expansion in the conventional sense. The Syndicate's intelligence division classified it, in a memo that has since been restricted to senior clearance, as "a coordinated infrastructure project masquerading as individual capability upgrades." What this means: the Automata Initiative is the runner coalition becoming a machine.

Hiram 0mission's fingerprints are on the design philosophy. The intelligence division has confirmed this with a confidence level of eighty-seven percent.

Ryo is back with Taka's Cookbook — a deck built around automated tool management, systems that trigger and reconfigure without manual input during the run window. Automation. The Cookbook runs itself. Ryo's role is to point it at a target and let the cookbook cook.

Machine politics in the Syndicate means something specific: the machinery of power, the mechanisms by which the Syndicate's influence is maintained. The runner coalition is attempting to build its own machine to counter it.

The Syndicate's policy development center in Lunacent's administrative district generates the internal policy documents that govern every subsidiary operation. If a runner can access the policy layer, they can understand the rules the Syndicate enforces on itself — the vulnerabilities built into the system by design, the exceptions written into policy by people who created exceptions for themselves.

An automated cookbook reading the policy documents.

Shut the kitchen.
