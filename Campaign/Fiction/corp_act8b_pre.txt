ACT OF WILL
by Desh Fuentes-Calloway

The runner in the file goes by Vic. No last name in the registry. No biographical annotation. The deck is Red Act — a name that carries connotations the intelligence division's cultural analysts have flagged as intentional. Red Act. An act of will, of principle, of deliberate choice.

The briefing note on Vic is shorter than most: *ideologically motivated. High commitment. Does not disengage under conventional economic pressure. Red Act is built for sustained engagement.*

This is the kind of runner the Syndicate finds difficult to model. The economic models that work for profit-motivated runners do not apply to principle-motivated ones. The credit drain that causes a profit runner to withdraw and recalculate causes a principle runner to find another way. The math is different when the calculation is not about money.

The Syndicate's legal processing center in the Lunacent commercial district handles contract adjudication for the entire Heinlein territory. Regulatory filings. Licensing disputes. The legal scaffolding that underlies every Syndicate operation. A runner inside the legal processing center can do more damage than a runner inside almost any other facility — not by stealing data but by examining it.

An act of will. A principled runner in a legal facility.

You will not be able to make this engagement not worth Vic's time. You can only make it impossible.

Make it impossible.

—
TARGET: Vic — Red Act
FACILITY: Syndicate Legal Processing Center, Lunacent Commercial District, Level 5
OBJECTIVE: Deny legal archive access. Protect contract adjudication infrastructure. Advance regulatory filing agendas.
