FUTURE CONTRACT: SEALED
by Orla Vaszquez-Nkosi

The accelerated cultivation reached operational phase at turn seven. The intelligence estimate had said seven or eight; it was seven. The Automata Initiative's optimization is real and the Syndicate's countermeasure team has noted that the window between the engagement starting and Future Freedom Flow becoming a critical threat has shrunk by approximately thirty-seven percent compared to the previous Fermented Future encounter.

Thirty-seven percent less time is a significant reduction.

The Syndicate had scored four agendas by turn seven.

The strategy that worked against the slower cultivation — aggressive early advancement, interrupt the credit engine before operational phase — worked here too, compressed into a shorter window. The pressure technique applied at turn four rather than turn five, the credit disruption timed to the faster acceleration curve. The disruption held the transition to operational phase by one additional turn.

One turn became two agendas.

Magdalene reached the contract registry at turn eleven, when the Future Freedom Flow configuration was operating at full capacity and the credit economics had stabilized enough to sustain a core run. The registry's inner ICE held. The outer ICE had been compromised; the inner layer was where the credit cost became prohibitive.

Final score: seven to four. Forward contract data unaccessed.

The future the Syndicate has contracted for remains private.

—
Future Freedom Flow flowered. It arrived too late.
The contract registry holds its contents sealed.
Seven agendas advanced. The futures are ours.
