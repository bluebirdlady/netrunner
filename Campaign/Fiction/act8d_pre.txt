RECOMBINATION
by Circuito Saudade

The biological research exemption was not in the framework documents. That was the first thing you noticed when you reread them after the NBN run.

The autonomous zone designation gave Weyland the extraction rights, Pravdivost the regulatory clearance, and NBN the media infrastructure. Jinteki's inclusion in the consortium had a different line item: "applied research facility." No further specification. No listed budget. The exemption category was left blank, with a reference code that did not resolve to any published Heinlein regulatory schedule.

The reference code resolved to something else.

A Jinteki research annex on Heinlein Station 9 had been operating for four years under a standard biomedical licence. Standard licence: genetic research, therapeutic applications, compliance with the Heinlein Bioethics Council review schedule. Four years of clean audits.

The annex had a secondary operation registered under the blank exemption category, using the same reference code from the framework. The secondary operation had never been reviewed.

The activist networks had a name for the kind of research Jinteki ran when no one was reviewing it. They called it "regenesis work." The name came from a project, three years ago, that a Jinteki whistleblower had described before going quiet. Attempting to rebuild capabilities that the bioethics conventions specifically prohibited. Working backwards from results to find a process that would be classified as something else.

The annex was where Jinteki was doing the biological part of Project Midnight Sun.

You needed to know what that meant before the vote.
