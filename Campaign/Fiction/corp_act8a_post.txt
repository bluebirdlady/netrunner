PRESSURE FRONT: WEATHERED
by Orla Vaszquez-Nkosi

The Cezve Revolver's credit cycling arrived faster than the probability models had projected. MuslihaT was operational at full pressure by turn four — a speed that the Syndicate's monitoring team noted as the fastest observed for this type of economy runner in the current threat tier. The pressure was real and it was sustained.

The routing hub's ICE held for six turns under conditions the engagement log characterizes as "continuous assault." There were no gaps in the pressure. MuslihaT ran a server every turn, cycling credits, applying force across multiple approach vectors simultaneously.

The communications infrastructure agendas had been advanced in the first three turns, before full pressure was established. This was the margin.

By turn seven, the routing hub's ICE had absorbed enough force that two outer subroutines required emergency patching. The credit reserve was nearly depleted. The Syndicate scored its seventh agenda point at turn seven while simultaneously losing a defensive asset that will require one maintenance cycle to replace.

MuslihaT scored three points. All three came through the left-flank approach vector, which the pressure had opened incrementally. The right flank held throughout.

The routing hub is operational. Communications processing resumed full capacity at 1347. The after-action report recommends augmented ICE on the left-flank approach vector before any future engagement.

The pressure front passed. Damage was done. The structure survived.
