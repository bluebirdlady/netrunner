CULTIVATION
by Temidayo Okonkwo-Bell

The name in the runner registry is Magdalene Keino-Chemutai. The deck is called Fermented Future. The analyst's note reads: *patience deck. Resource accumulation over extended timeline. Magdalene does not rush.*

Cultivation as a philosophy. You build and tend and wait for the conditions to ripen. Fermented Future is built around this principle — assets that appreciate over time, tools that become more valuable the longer the engagement lasts, a credit and resource engine that grows quietly in the background while the Syndicate focuses elsewhere.

The problem is the Syndicate is not focusing elsewhere. It is focused precisely on Magdalene Keino-Chemutai, on a runner whose presence in the coalition intelligence briefings has been flagged three times in the past two months. Not a flashy operator. Not a highly visible threat. The kind of threat that is dangerous specifically because it is easy to underestimate.

The Syndicate's agricultural research station on the Lunacent outskirts handles food science contracts for six colony support subsidiaries. Long-term contracts. The kind that appreciate over time. The kind that a patience-deck runner would find particularly interesting.

Your briefing note ends with a word the briefing officer appears to have deliberated over: *interrupt.* Not stop, not block, not deny. Interrupt.

You cannot let Fermented Future ripen. Find the engagement's pressure point and apply it before the cultivation completes.

—
TARGET: Magdalene Keino-Chemutai — Fermented Future
FACILITY: Syndicate Agricultural Research Station, Lunacent Outskirts, Bio-Station 2
OBJECTIVE: Interrupt patience engine before resource threshold. Advance food science contract agendas. Deny cultivation window.
