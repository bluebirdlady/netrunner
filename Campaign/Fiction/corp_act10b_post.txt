HOWLING: MUFFLED
by Nneka Adewale-Mortimer

Midnight Wolf's coordination layer was detectable. The monitoring team identified the data transmission signatures in the first two minutes of the engagement — not the content of the transmissions, the fact of them. Loup was broadcasting the engagement to the coalition's analysis network in real time, feeding the Syndicate's defensive responses to a team somewhere in the Lunar network that was processing the information and returning adjusted approach recommendations.

A runner with a live analyst team. The Syndicate has engaged with this threat profile exactly once before.

The response was to rotate the defensive configuration mid-engagement, twice, at irregular intervals — not to defeat the coordination layer but to introduce enough chaos that the real-time analysis being transmitted could not generate reliable recommendations faster than the rotation changed the situation being analyzed. The transmissions continued. The recommendations they generated were delayed, and delayed recommendations in a fast engagement become wrong recommendations.

The coordination core held. Scheduling data was not accessed.

Loup scored three points through runs where the rotation had temporarily created exposure — inevitable costs of the rotation strategy. The Syndicate scored seven, the final two agendas advanced in a window where the coordination layer's analysis lag was at its longest.

The howling continued throughout. The pack heard something, but what they heard was shaped by the Syndicate's rotations.

The signal they received was not fully accurate.

—
The wolf howled. The pack heard echoes, not the truth.
Coordination met disruption. The scheduling data was not transmitted.
The Syndicate's rhythm stays private. The pack must guess.
