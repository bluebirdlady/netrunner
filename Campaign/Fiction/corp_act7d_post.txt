TIDE WARNING: HIGH WATER MARK
by Ivette Serrano-Park

The momentum threshold arrived at turn twelve. The Syndicate had been told eleven to thirteen, and the analysts were correct — at turn twelve, Tide of Omore's resource engine crossed the level at which the probability models shift against the defender. For three turns following the threshold, the water reclamation facility operated under conditions the after-action report describes as "defensive emergency."

The Syndicate had scored six points before turn twelve.

The seventh point was scored at turn fourteen, under conditions that the engagement log records simply as "narrow." The credit reserve was depleted. Two ICE subroutines had been overloaded by the momentum press. One server had been accessed successfully by Topan in the threshold period and an agenda had been stolen. The final score: Syndicate seven, runner four.

Seven to four at high water. The water reclamation facility is intact.

Topan Ormas disconnected at turn fifteen. The momentum built by Tide of Omore dissipated with the disconnection — it is a deck that requires active pressure to maintain, and the pressure had pushed the facility to its limit but had not broken it.

The after-action report's closing note: *Recommend acceleration protocol for all future Topan Ormas engagements. The tide analogy is accurate. Do not let it rise.*

The tide receded. The facility processes water this morning as it did yesterday morning.

—
High water came. The Syndicate held the line at seven to four.
Narrow victories are still victories.
Water flows. Sector 7 operational. The tide went out.
