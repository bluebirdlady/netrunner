UPSWING
by Nneka Adewale-Mortimer

The Uprising expansion changes things. That is the word from Intelligence, delivered without elaboration, which means everyone in the briefing room understands that elaboration would take longer than the operational window allows.

What has changed: the runners have better tools. Coordinated acquisition channels — not corporate, not black market in the classic sense, but a network of sympathetic fabricators and open-source engineering collectives that have, in the span of eight weeks, produced a generation of rig components that the Syndicate's countermeasure team is still characterizing.

Zahya Sadeghi runs Lucky Switch. The briefing note is three lines: *Zahya Sadeghi. Shapers with a lucky streak. Credits accumulate through access events. The more runs, the richer. Stop the runs.*

The logic is circular in the way that the best runner strategies tend to be: she runs to get credits to run better. You have to interrupt the cycle early, before the credit engine reaches a self-sustaining threshold.

The Syndicate's Lunacent commercial hub is a glass-and-steel column rising through four transit levels. It handles licensing intake for eighty-six Syndicate subsidiaries. Clean, busy, above suspicion. Exactly the kind of facility that becomes a target when runners start feeling confident.

Zahya is feeling confident. The Uprising has that effect on people.

Your job is to remind her what the Syndicate feels like when it pushes back.

—
TARGET: Zahya Sadeghi — Lucky Switch
FACILITY: Syndicate Commercial Hub, Lunacent Transit Level 3
OBJECTIVE: Interrupt runner credit cycle. Advance licensing agendas. Deny repeated server access.
