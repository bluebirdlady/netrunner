THE PRICE OF RAGE
by Orla Vaszquez-Nkosi

Ryo Phoenix Ono's incident file is three pages of incident reports and one page of psychological assessment, and the psychological assessment is the most alarming part.

The assessment describes a runner who treats every corporate server as a personal affront. Who does not plan so much as accelerate — burning through ICE on pure aggression, trading resources for speed, accepting damage to keep moving forward. The classification in the file is "Anger Issues (Anarch variant, escalated)." The assessor has underlined the word "escalated."

What Ryo wants from the Syndicate Comms Center is unclear. The Center handles encrypted message routing for internal Syndicate communications — executive correspondence, operational directives, classified asset transfers. If a runner could maintain a tap here, they could read the Syndicate's internal voice.

But Ryo is not subtle enough for a tap. Ryo is going to hit the Comms Center with everything they have, burn whatever gets in the way, and grab whatever they can hold on the way through.

That is, in some ways, easier to defend against than Tao's lateral restructuring or Loup's patient viral saturation. Raw aggression has a predictable trajectory.

Build the walls thick. Let Ryo hit them. Count the costs. Make sure the runner's costs are higher than yours.

TARGET: Ryo Phoenix Ono — Anger Issues
FACILITY: Syndicate Comms Center, Lunacent Executive Ring
OBJECTIVE: Defend all servers. Deny access to executive communications. Outlast the runner's resource burn.
