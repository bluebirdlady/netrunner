ARMS RACE
by Ivette Serrano-Park

Zahya has upgraded.

The credentials spoofing that worked at Vault 7 — that gap is closed. Zahya knows it, because they stopped trying to use it six operational cycles ago. What shows up in the new access traces is a rig that has been substantially rebuilt. New bypass architectures. A suite of hardware that doesn't match the standard Criminal loadout. More expensive. More capable.

Someone is funding Zahya. Runners at this tier don't upgrade on their own credit — not this fast. There is a client behind the current engagement, and that client has resources.

The Syndicate Deep Facility is not on any public registry. It operates under three shell identities and maintains its network address behind a randomized relay chain. The facility houses the Syndicate's hardware fabrication contracts — the physical substrate for Project Crow's infrastructure layer. Not data. Physical. The kind of thing that, if a runner gets access to the fabrication specifications, can be reverse-engineered, replicated, or sold to the highest bidder.

Zahya's client almost certainly already knows the Deep Facility exists. The Getaway contracts in Zahya's recent history suggest a runner specializing in high-extraction jobs — in, out, clean, fast.

ICE every server. Make every access point expensive. The fabrication specs cannot leave this facility.

TARGET: Zahya — Getaway
FACILITY: Syndicate Deep Facility, Lunacent Sublevel 12
OBJECTIVE: Protect hardware fabrication contracts. Deny all server access. Expose runner's backing.
