SECOND STUDY

PT Untaian calls itself a pharmaceutical research company. Their public portfolio is reassuringly ordinary: anti-rejection compounds for organ printing, longevity supplements for the Belt's aging population, a line of agricultural-grade gene therapy products distributed across Heinlein's equatorial farming zones. Their literature describes their substrate work as "mycele-adjacent" — a phrase that means nothing specific and implies proximity to Jinteki's more publicized developments in biological architecture without making any claim the regulatory bodies could act on.

The gene therapy products are the part you've been staring at for three hours.

---

Project Pale Horse documented something called PTR VI. The Black Site records described it in language that was precise and unhurried — the way people write when they are certain no one outside the room will ever read it. PTR VI was characterized as a "targeted expression vector." The host range data filled forty pages.

H-982 was the production facility. PT Untaian supplied the substrate.

In the transfer records from the Zwicky Group, the medical supply deliveries to H-982 came from a PT Untaian subsidiary called Agri-Line Holdings. Agricultural gene therapy. Routine, if you don't know what you're looking at.

---

You've been running long enough to know that the cleanest-looking operations are worth the most to the people running them. PT Untaian's equatorial ring facility is spotless on paper. No violations. No incident reports. A model subsidiary filing quarterly reports and paying their infrastructure taxes six months early.

Their research division does not publish.

---

H-982's records documented test cohorts organized by "substrate batch." The batch numbers cross-reference to AU Co. agricultural unit production runs — the same agricultural clonelines that PT Untaian has been supplying with gene therapy products for two years.

You've been trying not to finish that thought for two days.

Someone has to go in and pull the active research logs before they're purged. The equatorial ring facility is live. Whatever the Black Site was building didn't stop when H-982 fell. It just moved somewhere quieter.

—

TARGET: PT Untaian Pharmaceutical
FACILITY: Heinlein Equatorial Ring Research Division
OBJECTIVE: Pull active project files. Find what PTR VI became after H-982.
