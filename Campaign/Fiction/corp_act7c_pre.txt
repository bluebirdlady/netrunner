THE PACK
by Desh Fuentes-Calloway

René "Loup" Arcemont. The handle means wolf in a language the Syndicate does not widely speak, and the intelligence division's cultural annotation notes this with the same dry precision it brings to everything: *a runner who has chosen a predator's name. Approach accordingly.*

Trickster Paladin is a social engineering deck — not purely technical. Loup's tools include elements that exploit the Syndicate's own personnel infrastructure: systems that intercept the human-facing layers of security, tools that create false access permissions in the windows between ICE layers. A runner who tricks rather than breaks.

This is a coalition player. The Syndicate's intelligence team has placed Loup in the inner circle of Hiram 0mission's network — not a contractor but a collaborator. Someone who knows the coalition's long-term objectives.

The Syndicate's personnel management center in Lunacent Sublevel 9 handles employee access credentials, performance reviews, internal audit reports. A social engineering specialist in the personnel system is a particular kind of nightmare: the damage is not in what they take but in what they can change, and changed access credentials persist beyond the engagement window.

"If Loup gets into the personnel core, we're not dealing with a single breach," your supervisor says. "We're dealing with a vector that stays open."

The pack hunts with the pack's strategy. A lone wolf in our systems is already not alone.

Shut the door before the pack comes through.

TARGET: René "Loup" Arcemont — Trickster Paladin
FACILITY: Syndicate Personnel Management Center, Lunacent Sublevel 9
