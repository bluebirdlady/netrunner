MIDNIGHT APPROACH
by Temidayo Okonkwo-Bell

The Midnight Sun expansion has changed the threat landscape in ways the Syndicate's analysts are still measuring. The summary briefing uses phrases like "elevated coordination" and "shared intelligence infrastructure" — language that acknowledges, without quite stating plainly, that the runners are no longer operating as isolated actors.

They are talking to each other. They are sharing what they learn.

Az McCaffrey is back. The file has been updated: Blueberry Breakers has been replaced by Prognostic Boomerang, a rig built around predictive analysis tools. McCaffrey doesn't just adapt mid-run now. McCaffrey predicts. The deck models the Syndicate's probable ICE configurations based on shared intelligence from the runner coalition and anticipates what will be behind which server before the run begins.

This is a different kind of problem.

The Syndicate's orbital monitoring station in the high Lunacent ring watches twenty-three satellite clusters. The strategic value of the station is intelligence — data feeds that the Syndicate uses to track competitor movements, regulatory filings, shipping manifests. If McCaffrey can run the station's archive with Prognostic Boomerang's predictive layer active, the Syndicate's own intelligence apparatus becomes transparent.

Your supervisor does not use the word scared. The word the supervisor uses is concerned. The word is delivered in a tone that functions as a synonym.

Do not let McCaffrey see inside the station.

—
TARGET: Az McCaffrey — Prognostic Boomerang
FACILITY: Syndicate Orbital Monitoring Station, Lunacent High Ring, Platform 7
OBJECTIVE: Deny archive access. Protect intelligence data feeds. Advance monitoring infrastructure agendas.
