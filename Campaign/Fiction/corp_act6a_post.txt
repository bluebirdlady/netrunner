UPSWING: ARRESTED
by Nneka Adewale-Mortimer

Zahya Sadeghi ran the commercial hub four times. Each run added credits. Each run built toward the threshold where Lucky Switch becomes impossible to contain — a self-reinforcing engine that the Syndicate's countermeasure models describe as "approaching parity with a well-funded corp economy by turn twelve."

The Syndicate did not give her twelve turns.

ICE was rezzed aggressively, in the opening window, earlier than standard doctrine recommends. The credit cost was significant. Intelligence reviewed the decision afterward and marked it as correct: you cannot let Lucky Switch breathe. The moment the credit engine achieves escape velocity, the engagement is decided.

Zahya hit server two on the fourth run and found three layers of ICE that had not been there on the second run. The commercial hub's architecture had been restructured mid-engagement, assets rerouted, the innermost server sealed behind a configuration she had not scanned.

She scored two points. The Syndicate scored seven. The licensing agendas resolved cleanly through the hub's transit infrastructure and were registered with the Commerce Authority before the cycle could reset.

The upswing, for now, belongs to the Syndicate.

For now.

—
The credit cycle was interrupted. The engine never fired.
Lucky Switch found no luck here today.
The commercial hub holds. Licensing proceeds.
