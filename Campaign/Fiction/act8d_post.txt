ITERATION

The secondary operation records were indexed under one word: REGENESIS.

Not a project name — a classification. A method. Jinteki had developed it across eleven distinct research cycles, each one classified as a separate therapeutic study, each one building on the last. Individually, each cycle passed bioethics review. Together, they constituted something the conventions specifically prohibited: a reversible identity-imprint process for bioroid units.

The process allowed a bioroid's core identity architecture — what made a specific unit that specific unit, its memory, its decision weight, its accumulated self — to be extracted, archived, and reinstalled. Into the same unit. Into a different chassis. Or, with the modifications the annex had been developing for the past eighteen months, into a clone substrate.

The blank exemption category in the Midnight Sun framework was for this. An autonomous zone with no bioethics review meant Jinteki could scale REGENESIS from eleven experimental cycles to full industrial production.

The Automata Initiative had established that bioroids and clones were products, not persons. REGENESIS, at scale, meant that any bioroid who had developed something worth preserving — skills, relationships, a way of understanding the world — could have that development extracted and archived before being decommissioned. Jinteki called it "continuity-as-a-service."

The activist networks had a different name for it.

You sent the records to the analyst and to Mercury's contact address. Then you found the thread that led to Haas-Bioroid. Because someone had to be building the hardware.
