FIRST DEFENSE
by Orla Vaszquez-Nkosi

The briefing room on sublevel three is smaller than you expected. One chair, one table, one screen. The Syndicate does not believe in excess — not in furniture, not in sentiment, not in mercy.

Your supervisor's voice arrives through a speaker you cannot locate. "Kaguya Data Vault went live forty-eight hours ago. We've already flagged two unauthorized access attempts on the outer subnet. Nothing landed. The runners out there are feeling us out, probing the edges, looking for a seam."

You have reviewed the access logs. The probes are unsophisticated, tentative. Someone new. Someone who doesn't know yet what they've stumbled against.

"We're giving you the standard loadout," the voice continues. "ICE, credits, assets. You'll learn what works. You'll learn what doesn't. The Vault holds pre-release pharmaceutical data from four subsidiaries. If any of that gets out before our licensing agreements close, we lose approximately sixty million in negotiating leverage. So."

The screen goes dark. That's all the pep talk you're getting.

You settle into the chair and pull up the Vault's architecture. Subroutines nest inside subroutines. Credits flow from R&D into the investment stack. The ice goes up slowly, methodically, like a wall being built one stone at a time.

The runner — they're calling themselves The Catalyst in the incident reports — will be back. They always come back.

Let them come.

—
TARGET: Convention Breaker — The Catalyst
FACILITY: Kaguya Data Vault, Lunacent Sublevel 7
OBJECTIVE: Defend all servers. Advance agendas. Do not let the runner score.
