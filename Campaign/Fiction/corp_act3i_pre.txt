FINAL QUARTER
by Orla Vaszquez-Nkosi

Floor 99 of Synapse Global Tower is not a Syndicate facility by any documentation that would survive external scrutiny. The tower itself belongs to a holding company. Floor 99 is leased to a consulting firm that exists in two jurisdictions and has no employees. The servers on Floor 99 belong to a network address registered in the outer belt.

Magdalene found it anyway.

Magdalene's profile is the most unsettling document in the threat assessment archive. Not because of what it describes her doing, but because of how little the Syndicate actually knows about her. Runner alias. No legal name on file. No credential trail that leads anywhere real. The runner boards know her by the name, by the style — architectural runner, they call the approach, someone who builds their intrusion tools the way an architect designs a building: for specific purposes, with structural elegance, with a view toward what the space needs to become.

She is called "Sabbatical" because, the runner community's collective wisdom suggests, she takes long breaks between major engagements. She has been on sabbatical. Now she is not.

Whatever Magdalene found — whatever thread, whatever chain of inference led her from the public record to Floor 99 — is the question that matters. Because if she found it once, she can find it again, and the servers on Floor 99 hold the Project Crow operational timeline.

Defend Floor 99. Score the operational timeline agendas. Do not let her read the calendar.

—
TARGET: Magdalene — Sabbatical
FACILITY: Synapse Global Tower, Floor 99, Heinlein High Orbit Tether District
OBJECTIVE: Protect Project Crow operational timeline. Score all remaining agendas. Seal the approach vector.
