PROFIT OVER PRINCIPLE
by Orla Vaszquez-Nkosi

Floor one hundred and two of the Syndicate Executive Suite has a view of all of Lunacent through the transplas dome. On a clear cycle you can see Earthshine catching the dust on the dome's outer surface, turning the whole ceiling silver.

You have not looked at the dome in three days.

The final briefing is delivered by the Syndicate's Executive Director — a person whose name you have never been given, who exists in your operational context only as a voice and a set of directives. The voice is tired in a way that well-managed people do not permit themselves to sound.

"MuslihaT has been retained for a campaign the runner community is calling 'Negative Marketing.' The intent is disruption. If the Syndicate's agendas cannot be secured in this window, competing interests will use MuslihaT's access to seed disinformation into our public-facing communications, challenge our licensing filings with the Commerce Authority, and initiate a coordinated media event tied to whatever Ryo's regulatory filing eventually surfaces."

Project Crow. The orbital infrastructure. The biomedical research. Four years of work in the Syndicate's deepest servers. Eighteen months from completion.

"Secure the Executive Suite. Score the remaining agenda points. The project completes or it does not, and if it does not, the Syndicate — as an entity — may not survive the regulatory and reputational consequences."

The final run. Defend everything.

—
TARGET: MuslihaT — Negative Marketing Campaign
FACILITY: Syndicate Executive Suite, Floor 102, Lunacent
OBJECTIVE: Score all remaining agenda points. Defend the Executive Suite. Complete Project Crow. Profit over principle.
