INVENTORY: CLEARED
by Desh Fuentes-Calloway

MuslihaT's approach was, as anticipated, blunt. The runner crashed through Zwicky Group's outer perimeter with the kind of credit-heavy brute-force methodology that cold storage firms do not typically need to defend against. Three Zwicky ICE layers fell in the first twenty minutes.

By then, the cold storage archive had been restructured. The sensitive agenda documents — the three legacy contracts flagged in the threat assessment — had been pulled from cold storage and transferred to a Syndicate-controlled secondary relay six hours before the run began. MuslihaT reached the archive shelf and found it empty.

What they grabbed instead: decommissioned employee performance reviews from a PT Untaian subsidiary that closed in 2048, an archive of expired pharmaceutical import licenses, and four hundred and twelve megabytes of internal cafeteria menu rotations.

The cafeteria menus would have been from the Heinlein food services contractor. The Syndicate has no idea what MuslihaT's buyer will make of them.

Zwicky Group filed a security incident report with their insurance provider. The Syndicate's legal team reviewed it and determined the Syndicate's liability was minimal.

—
MuslihaT looted a cleared shelf.
The sensitive material was already gone.
Whatever their buyer paid, they overpaid.
