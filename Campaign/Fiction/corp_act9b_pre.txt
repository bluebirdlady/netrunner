IRON HARVEST
by Ivette Serrano-Park

Esa Afontov has escalated. The intelligence file has been updated three times in the past two weeks, each update reflecting a capability increase that the analysts are tracking with increasing urgency. Steeling Keiko was a hybrid build. Tungsten Carnivore is something different — a designation that the threat profile describes without diplomatic softening as "aggressive high-strength ICE destruction deck at coalition-augmented capability level."

Tungsten is harder than steel. A carnivore consumes what it breaks.

The Automata Initiative has given Esa tools that the previous Syndicate countermeasure library was not designed to handle. The briefing officer lists them: three new ICE-breaking suites, two automated kill programs, a recursive destruction loop that can systematically dismantle an ICE configuration given enough time. The briefing officer delivers this list in a flat voice that is performing calm.

The Syndicate's heavy industry research center in the Heinlein industrial zone holds the engineering specifications for the orbital construction platforms that underpin Project Crow's physical infrastructure. The specs exist in the Syndicate's system because they have to be there for the engineering teams to work from. They cannot be removed. They can only be protected.

Iron harvest: the debris left after a battle. Esa is coming to create debris.

The Syndicate needs to make sure the debris is not structural.

—
TARGET: Esa Afontov — Tungsten Carnivore
FACILITY: Syndicate Heavy Industry Research Center, Heinlein Industrial Zone, Engineering Core
OBJECTIVE: Protect engineering specifications against high-strength ICE destruction. Advance infrastructure agendas. Deny carnivore access to core.
