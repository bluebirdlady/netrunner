STEEL AND SOIL
by Ivette Serrano-Park

Esa Afontov. The name has appeared in the Syndicate's coalition intelligence briefings twice in the past three weeks, each time in the context of escalating runner activity in Heinlein's industrial sector. The deck is Steeling Keiko — a name with a personal resonance the intelligence division has not been able to fully document, which means it was chosen for a reason the file doesn't contain.

Steeling Keiko is a hybrid configuration: technical strength combined with sustained resilience. Not pure speed, not pure patience, but a blend — tools that build toward a hardened state, a rig that becomes progressively more difficult to displace as the engagement advances. The longer Esa runs, the harder Esa becomes to stop.

The Syndicate's mining operations coordination center in the Heinlein lower ring manages the logistics contracts for asteroid extraction across the entire territorial claim. Steel comes from the asteroids. The Syndicate's territory exists because the asteroids were claimed, processed, and built into this world.

"Esa has connections in the mining labor community," the briefing officer says. This is information the Syndicate's communications team has been managing for six weeks. Runners with community connections run a different kind of risk — they run for audiences, and audiences create records.

Soil and steel. The Moon has none of one and the Syndicate has built a world from the other.

Defend that world. Defend it quickly.

—
TARGET: Esa Afontov — Steeling Keiko
FACILITY: Syndicate Mining Operations Coordination Center, Heinlein Lower Ring, Hub 6
OBJECTIVE: Advance mining logistics agendas before runner hardens. Deny coordination center access. Protect extraction contracts.
