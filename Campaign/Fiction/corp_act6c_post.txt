STRUCTURAL DAMAGE: SURVIVED
by Orla Vaszquez-Nkosi

The damage was real. By the midpoint of the engagement, three rezzed assets had been trashed and one ICE subroutine had been deactivated — not broken through, deactivated, left as a dead node in the server architecture that the Syndicate's reconstruction team will need two maintenance cycles to restore.

Demolished performs exactly as advertised. Topan Ormas is methodical about destruction in a way that is almost aesthetic.

But the mineral contracts had already advanced.

The Syndicate's strategy, in retrospect, was correct if uncomfortable: accept some demolition, accelerate agenda advancement, score before the core servers become unreachable. It is a strategy that requires tolerating damage while remaining functional. The after-action report characterizes this as "controlled sacrifice." Intelligence will review whether the maintenance costs justify the outcome.

The outcome: seven agenda points scored. Two points conceded when Topan found a server that had been stripped of its outer ICE. The materials processing center is structurally degraded but operational.

Topan disconnected at 1401, having left the facility noticeably smaller than it was at engagement start.

The mineral contracts are registered. The facility will be rebuilt. Topan will return with updated demolition tools.

The Syndicate notes this and allocates a reconstruction budget.

—
The facility survived. Barely is still surviving.
Demolished found damage but not victory.
Mineral contracts registered. Reconstruction begins at 0600.
