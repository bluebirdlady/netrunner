TIDE WARNING
by Ivette Serrano-Park

Topan Ormas is back. The intelligence file has been updated: Demolished has been replaced by Tide of Omore. This is a meaningful change. The Syndicate's analysts reviewed the new configuration overnight and the briefing note carries an unusual qualifier: *significantly more dangerous than previous encounter.*

Tide of Omore is not a destruction deck. It is a momentum deck. Where Demolished methodically dismantled, Tide of Omore builds toward an overwhelming force — tools that compound, resources that accumulate, run windows that widen as the engagement progresses. The tide rises. If it rises far enough, there is no ICE configuration adequate to hold it.

The timing warning in the briefing note reads: *engagement must be decided before the runner's momentum threshold. Estimated threshold: turn eleven to thirteen. After this point, probability of successful defense declines below forty percent.*

Eleven turns. Thirteen at the outside.

The Syndicate's water reclamation facility in the Lunacent outer ring handles the most fundamental infrastructure on the Moon: water processing for the civilian population of the Heinlein territory. The symbolic significance of a runner coalition targeting water infrastructure is not lost on the Syndicate's communications team, who have been briefed separately and told not to communicate about it externally.

The tide is coming. The facility handles life support systems.

This is not the time for a long engagement.

—
TARGET: Topan Ormas — Tide of Omore
FACILITY: Syndicate Water Reclamation Facility, Lunacent Outer Ring, Sector 7
OBJECTIVE: Score agendas before momentum threshold. Deny tide build-up. Protect water infrastructure.
