FUTURE CONTRACT
by Orla Vaszquez-Nkosi

Magdalene Keino-Chemutai is back. Fermented Future has been augmented — the patience engine that took time to interrupt in the previous engagement has been tightened, the threshold accelerated, the cultivation window shortened by the Automata Initiative's optimization layer. Where Fermented Future previously took twelve turns to ripen, the updated configuration is estimated to reach operational phase by turn seven or eight.

The intelligence note's final line: *patience that requires less patience is significantly more dangerous.*

Future Freedom Flow is the designation for the updated configuration — not Fermented Future anymore, something with a different philosophy written into the name. Freedom. The cultivation is no longer building toward an economic objective. It is building toward something the coalition has chosen to call freedom, which is the kind of word that appears in a runner configuration name when the runner has moved from professional engagement to ideological one.

Magdalene was always patient. Magdalene is now patient in the service of a cause.

The Syndicate's futures trading center in Lunacent's financial district handles the forward contracts that give the Syndicate leverage over territory and resources not yet developed. Future contracts are the Syndicate's most powerful tool for pre-emptive claim. If the coalition's analysis teams can access the future contract registry, they can see where the Syndicate intends to expand before the expansion happens.

The future the Syndicate has contracted for must remain private.

—
TARGET: Magdalene Keino-Chemutai — Future Freedom Flow
FACILITY: Syndicate Futures Trading Center, Lunacent Financial District, Contract Registry
OBJECTIVE: Protect forward contract data. Interrupt accelerated cultivation engine. Advance futures infrastructure agendas.
