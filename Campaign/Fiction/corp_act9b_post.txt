IRON HARVEST: COSTLY
by Ivette Serrano-Park

The damage assessment for this engagement is the longest the Syndicate has produced for a single facility in the current operational period. Four ICE subroutines destroyed — not deactivated, not overloaded, destroyed. The heavy industry research center's outer and middle defensive layers will require three maintenance cycles and significant credit expenditure to restore. The engineering teams working inside the center were temporarily displaced while the ICE reconstruction begins.

The engineering specifications were not accessed.

Tungsten Carnivore is as described. Esa Afontov reached the inner defensive layer — the layer directly preceding the engineering core — before the credit cost of destroying three subroutines in sequence exhausted the run economy. The recursive destruction loop ran twice. Both cycles completed. The third cycle began and did not complete because the credit reserve the loop requires was gone.

The engineering core held.

The Syndicate scored seven agendas. Five in the early window, before the carnivore configuration reached full operational pressure. Two in the late window, during the period when the destruction economy had depleted and the pressure dropped below critical. The late-window advances cost assets that were left deliberately exposed to draw engagement time away from the core.

Esa scored five points. This is the highest single-engagement runner score in the current campaign.

Five points. Seven points. The margin is two. The after-action report does not call this comfortable.
