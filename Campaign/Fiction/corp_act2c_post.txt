TOPOLOGY: STABLE
by Nneka Adewale-Mortimer

The diagnostic terminal was patched at 0800. By 0900 Tao had found a second peripheral seam — a legacy sync port from an earlier hardware generation that the architecture team had flagged for removal and then forgotten about. By 0930, that was patched too.

Tao spent the rest of the session playing catch-up, chasing scoring windows that kept moving before their rig could reshape the approach. The biosynthesis data moved to escrow in three clean transfers. By the time the final research agenda resolved, the Research Station's topology looked nothing like it had at the start of the session — three emergency reinforcements, two ICE reinstalls, one complete server resequencing.

It cost credits. It cost time. But the data moved.

In the post-incident summary, Network Architect Fumiko Szymanski writes: "Runner demonstrated advanced structural intuition. Recommend a full peripheral audit within thirty days."

She schedules it for twenty.
