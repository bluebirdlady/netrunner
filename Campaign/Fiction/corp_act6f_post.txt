FLUID ARCHITECTURE: FROZEN
by Nneka Adewale-Mortimer

Tāo found two of the four unpatched gaps. The after-action report is frank about this: two architectural vulnerabilities that the Syndicate's maintenance cycle had not closed were identified and exploited within the first four minutes of the engagement. Daily Plasticity is not guessing. Tāo has a systematic approach for structural identification that the intelligence division will need time to fully characterize.

Through those two gaps, Tāo scored four points. This is significant. This is the highest score a runner has achieved in a single engagement at a Heinlein commercial facility in the current quarter.

The Syndicate scored seven.

The margin is smaller than previous engagements. The licensing agendas advanced cleanly — three in the first window, two in the second, the final two pushed through while Tāo was pressing the third gap and finding it sealed. The gap-closing protocol consumed significant credits but the investment held: the third and fourth unpatched vulnerabilities were reinforced mid-engagement, and Daily Plasticity's fluid approach could not reshape access through a gap that no longer existed.

The licensing center is intact. The licensing agendas are registered. The four gaps are now, as of maintenance cycle 2247, all patched.

Tāo Salonga will find the next set of gaps. They always do.

Fluid architecture, frozen at the critical moment.
Four points conceded. Seven scored. The margin shrinks.
Licensing registered. The gaps are closed. Until they aren't.
