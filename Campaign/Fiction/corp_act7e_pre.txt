TRUE NORTH
by Nneka Adewale-Mortimer

Captain Padma Isbister. The title is literal: she commands a crew, which is unusual in a field populated by solo operators and small cells. The intelligence file on Isbister is longer than most and begins with a line that the analyst appears to have written carefully: *a runner who thinks institutionally.*

Threading is a logistics deck. Not destruction, not momentum, not social engineering — logistics. Isbister uses tools that map and optimize access routes, that identify the shortest path from the outer subnet to the target server, that strip away the unnecessary steps that a less organized runner would take. Threading means efficiency through planning. It means knowing where you're going before you arrive.

A captain who thinks institutionally. A logistics deck that plans the route. The Syndicate's intelligence team has placed Isbister in the runner coalition's operational planning function — she is not the strategist but she executes the strategy. When Hiram 0mission needs something specific from a specific place, Isbister is how it gets retrieved.

The Syndicate's navigation data archive in the Heinlein high orbital station holds route charts for the entire Lunar near-space. Shipping routes, orbital mechanics, transfer trajectories. Data that a logistics-minded captain would find extremely useful.

True north for a ship is the direction it intends to travel. Find out what direction Isbister is pointed before she gets there.

—
TARGET: Captain Padma Isbister — Threading
FACILITY: Syndicate Navigation Data Archive, Heinlein High Orbital Station, Deck 4
OBJECTIVE: Deny route chart access. Protect orbital navigation data. Advance navigation infrastructure agendas.
