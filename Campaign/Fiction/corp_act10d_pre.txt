WEARABLE TECH
by Orla Vaszquez-Nkosi

Loup again. The intelligence division's notation for a runner appearing in three consecutive act-tier engagements is a double mark — a flag within a flag, indicating that the runner's operational tempo has reached a level that suggests direct coalition command involvement rather than freelance assignment.

Loup is being directed by Hiram 0mission. The Syndicate is confident of this.

This Devil Wears Chisel is the third configuration the Syndicate has faced from René Arcemont. Each iteration has added a layer. Trickster Paladin was social engineering. Midnight Wolf was coordination. This Devil Wears Chisel is integration — a configuration where the social engineering layer, the coordination layer, and new Rebellion without Rehearsal augmentations operate simultaneously as a single unified system.

The name comes from somewhere the intelligence division can't locate. Chisel. A precision tool for removing material. Something that fits in the hand and creates a shape from what was formless.

The Syndicate's hardware development center in the Heinlein technology district produces the physical components that underpin Syndicate ICE architecture — the wearable tech that keeps Syndicate personnel secure in the field, the chips that form the foundation of every server. Hardware. The physical layer beneath everything.

A precision tool aimed at the hardware that makes the Syndicate work.

The chisel must not find purchase.

—
TARGET: René "Loup" Arcemont — This Devil Wears Chisel
FACILITY: Syndicate Hardware Development Center, Heinlein Technology District, Component Core
OBJECTIVE: Deny precision access to hardware specifications. Protect component architecture. Advance hardware development agendas.
