QUARANTINE SUCCESSFUL
by Temidayo Okonkwo-Bell

Devourer's first payload entered the Platform's outer subnet at 0347. The ICE identified it in 0.4 seconds and began the subroutine compartmentalization sequence. The payload ate three layers of routing architecture before the firewall quarantine sealed around it, and then it ate those too.

But by then it was eating itself. Contained, the Devourer payload had nothing new to consume and began cycling back through its own code strings. Network Architecture Lead Cristovao Mendes watched it collapse on the monitoring display and said, very quietly, "Huh."

The orbital relay agenda scored at 0412. A second followed at 0431. The Platform's physical antenna arrays never went offline — the Devourer had been aimed at the software layer, and the hardware had been air-gapped before the session began.

Loup's disconnect came at 0445, viral suite depleted, credit reserve empty.

The shell company chain that led them to the Platform is being reviewed. Three additional layers of intermediary are being added.

The Platform holds.
The relay network is intact.
Devourer learned, too late, that some architectures are built to contain fires.
