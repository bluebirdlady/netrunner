ALONE IN THE JUNGLE
by Circuito Saudade

The continuity candidate's shipping record terminated at a campus you had not heard of: Méliès U, "the most prestigious university in the Heinlein Belt," according to its own marketing. Its research division, according to everyone else, did not publish — not because it had nothing to show, but because nothing it produced was ever attributed to a name.

The campus itself was strange in person, or strange in the satellite imagery you pulled, which was the closest you were going to get. Half of it ran like any other research campus: lecture halls, transit lines, a visible population. The other half — the half that had received the continuity candidate — registered as nearly empty. Building access logs, environmental systems, transit routing: all of it sized for a population of thousands, all of it currently serving fewer than a dozen.

A campus built for a community that had mostly left, still running at full operating cost, for whoever remained.

You found one access log entry for the continuity candidate, dated the day of arrival. A single line: assigned — independent research, unsupervised, indefinite. No department. No advisor. No further entries since.

Whoever Mercury had become, Méliès U had given them a campus to disappear into. You were going to have to go in after them — through a research division that did not publish, in buildings that, on paper, were not supposed to have anyone left inside them.

—

TARGET: Méliès U: Only the Brightest
FACILITY: Méliès U Closed Research Campus, Heinlein Outer Ring
OBJECTIVE: Find the continuity candidate's research assignment. Find out what "independent research, unsupervised, indefinite" has produced in three months.
