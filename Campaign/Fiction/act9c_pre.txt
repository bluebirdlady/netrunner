RECTANGLE THEORY
by Circuito Saudade

AU Co.'s recipient account fed into something called "Creche Annex Thirty-Nine."

AU Co. sold clones the way other companies sold heirlooms — small batches, long waitlists, a CEO who spoke about gengineering in the language of horticulture. Their public crèches were showpieces: climate-controlled, glass-walled, open for viewing by appointment. Annex Thirty-Nine was not on the viewing schedule. It was not on any schedule. It appeared in AU Co.'s facilities registry as a "decommissioned development site," last active fourteen years ago, currently drawing power, water, and a nutrient-line allocation consistent with active crèche operations for approximately sixty individuals.

Sixty. Forty-four reassigned to other Earth Station facilities, seventeen sold to GameNET as biometric source material. Sixty-one names in the original batch.

One name unaccounted for.

You pulled Annex Thirty-Nine's resource allocation history as far back as it went. Fourteen years of "decommissioned," and fourteen years of power draw that never dropped to zero. Someone had been keeping the lights on in an empty facility for fourteen years, and had started running crèche-grade nutrient lines through it the same week Earth Station's clearing account first activated.

Whatever Thirty-Nine had been counting, it had started a long time before this batch.
