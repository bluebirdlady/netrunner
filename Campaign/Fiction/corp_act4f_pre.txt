DAMAGE CONTROL
by Desh Fuentes-Calloway

Phoenix Rising Commune is not on any corporate map. It is three hundred people living in a converted transit depot in Heinlein's outer ring, running on solar tap and collective labor, maintaining a network node that is registered under a community land trust and governed by a charter that predates the Lunar Commerce Authority's jurisdiction expansion.

Ryo Phoenix Ono lives there. When Ryo is not running.

The monitoring team has been watching the Commune for six weeks, ever since the Comms Center engagement suggested that Ryo's anger was not purely impulsive — that there was a community behind the fury, a group of people whose situation the Syndicate's operations had materially worsened in ways the Syndicate's legal team is still assessing.

"Regulation Breaker" is Ryo's self-description now. Not just Anger Issues anymore. Something that has acquired a name, a shape, a sense of its own purpose.

The Phoenix Rising server contains everything Ryo has gathered: three hundred people's testimony about what the Syndicate's Comms Array routing priority policies did to their network access, their employment applications, their healthcare submissions. The human cost of decisions that were, from the Syndicate's perspective, operational optimizations.

That testimony, formatted and submitted to the Lunar Commerce Authority, opens a regulatory investigation.

Score the remaining agendas before Ryo finishes the filing.

—
TARGET: Ryo Phoenix Ono — Regulation Breaker
FACILITY: Phoenix Rising Commune, Heinlein Outer Ring
OBJECTIVE: Reach and delay Ryo's regulatory filing. Score final agendas. Contain the testimony.
