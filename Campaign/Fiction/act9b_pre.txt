FLOOD THE MARKET
by Circuito Saudade

The GameNET recipient account was named, with a bluntness that almost felt like a joke, "Talent Acquisition — Special Sourcing."

GameNET's public face was relentless: a saturation broadcaster, every channel running simultaneous feeds, every feed running advertisements for things you had not asked about and would now never stop seeing. "Where Dreams are Real" was less a slogan than an operating principle — GameNET manufactured demand the way other corporations manufactured product, and the demand, once manufactured, became real enough to bill against.

"Special Sourcing" did not appear in any GameNET org chart you could find. It appeared exactly once, in a budget reconciliation document, as a line item that absorbed the transfers from Earth Station's clearing account and converted them — through a chain of shell purchases — into participant contracts for something called the Magistrate Revontulet Initiative.

You could not find what the Magistrate Revontulet Initiative was. You could find that it had a recruitment quota, that the quota was being met entirely from Earth Station's "reassignment" pool, and that GameNET's broadcast division was already running promotional content for it — content that named no participants, showed no faces, and aired, repeatedly, to an audience metric that GameNET's own systems flagged as statistically impossible.

People were watching something that, as far as you could tell, had not happened yet.
