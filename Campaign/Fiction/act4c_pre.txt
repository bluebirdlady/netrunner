CONVERGENCE

AU Co. guarantees their agricultural units to a stated specification. The marketing language is cheerful about this: reliable, consistent, purpose-optimized. The brochures show clone workers in Heinlein's low-gravity farms, smiling in the way that means the expression was designed rather than earned.

They are, by their own accounting, the gold standard in clone production. Their premium lines are well-documented — historical and cultural figures reconstructed at considerable expense, marketed to institutions as "living heritage." You've seen coverage of one of those programs. A panel of reconstructed historical philosophers debating corporate ethics for a media company, their creator watching from off-stage. You remember thinking it was the most honest thing a corporation had ever accidentally produced.

The agricultural lines are less publicized. Nine thousand units per quarter. Shipped to customers across six inhabited bodies.

The batch numbers in the PRH-II files match AU Co. production codes.

---

Somewhere in the Heinlein agricultural zones, AU Co.'s natal division is processing substrate, running viability checks, issuing conditioning protocols, and shipping finished product on a schedule that has not changed in two years. The Zwicky transfers funded it. PT Untaian supplied the modified substrate. The natal division's quality assurance team signed off on every batch.

They may not have known what they were signing.

You have been trying to locate a runner named BONES for four days. An old contact name from a Zwicky dead drop record, connected to a newsie who went quiet. You found a handle, a burned account, and three messages that all say some version of: I know. I can't help you. Don't reach out again.

You understand. You're reaching out anyway.

BONES hasn't responded.

---

The natal division records are air-gapped from the research archive. AU Co. separates production data from R&D as standard liability architecture. You'll need to pull the production side and run the cross-reference yourself.

If the link exists — if the agricultural units being shipped across six inhabited bodies carry PRH-II's expression vector — then this stopped being a research program a long time ago.

You go in anyway.
