CONTAINED
by Desh Fuentes-Calloway

Ryo did not wait for the Syndicate to come to them. The Phoenix Rising server node went live at 0600 and Ryo was inside the Syndicate's outer network at 0603, running everything they had left, burning faster than any previous engagement — all anger and no calculation, exactly what the assessor had described, exactly what the defense was prepared for.

The regulatory filing was half-assembled when the Commune's server encountered the Syndicate's defensive package. The monitoring team had obtained the Lunar Commerce Authority's submission protocol specifications; the defensive package included a subroutine specifically designed to introduce errors into submission formatting that would result in a processing delay.

Not a deletion. A delay. The legal team had been very specific about the distinction.

The testimony exists. Three hundred people's accounts, formatted and ready. The submission delay buys the Syndicate's legal team an estimated sixty days to prepare a counter-filing.

The agendas scored at 0712 and 0744. Ryo disconnected at 0748, the filing queued but not submitted, the error subroutine embedded in the formatting layer.

The Syndicate's legal team is already drafting. They have sixty days.

—
Ryo is still in the Commune. The filing is still in the queue.
The testimony doesn't go away. It waits.
Sixty days is not a victory — it is the last margin. Use it.
