REALITY, PLUS
by Circuito Saudade

NBN had a saying, internal and unofficial: "The story is always true. We just decide when."

NBN: Reality Plus was the division that decided when. Reality Plus managed narrative scheduling for Heinlein's fourteen licensed news channels, three independent holofeeds that were independent in name only, and the civic information system that populated every public screen on every station. They did not create news. They created the context in which news was received.

The Midnight Sun framework needed context.

You had found it in Pravdivost's records: a media strategy contract, subcontracted to Reality Plus, for a "public information campaign" timed to coincide with the final regulatory approvals. The campaign's objective, per the contract brief: "normalise autonomous zone designation as a natural extension of existing corporate infrastructure, pre-empt negative regulatory discourse, and ensure that council approval reads as consensus rather than controversy."

In other words: by the time the vote happened, the public would already believe it had been inevitable.

The campaign assets were being staged in a Reality Plus production facility on Heinlein Station 7. Three weeks of content. Fifty-six pieces of pre-approved coverage. A synthetic opinion poll. The poll results had been finalised before any polling had taken place.

The production facility's internal network was connected to every content delivery system in Heinlein. That connection went both ways.

—

TARGET: NBN: Reality Plus
FACILITY: Reality Plus Production Hub, Heinlein Station 7, Media Quarter
OBJECTIVE: Pull the campaign assets and the poll data. Find the full content schedule for the public rollout. And if the delivery system connection goes both ways — consider what else it could deliver.
