STREET GALLERY
by Desh Fuentes-Calloway

Arissana Rocha Nahu. The name appears for the first time in the Syndicate's active runner registry, transferred from a watch list to a threat tier following three Automata Initiative engagements that the intelligence division classified as demonstrating "coalition-level strategic coordination." Arissana is not acting alone.

The Lilypad Gamble. The deck name is almost whimsical and the intelligence briefing note does not let this impression stand: *deceptively playful designation for a high-commitment sabotage configuration built around public exposure objectives.* The Lilypad Gamble is designed to create public incidents — runs that generate visible evidence of Syndicate facility operation, runs that produce artifacts the coalition can display rather than simply exploit.

Street gallery. The briefing officer says the phrase and lets the implication settle: Arissana intends to create an exhibition from what she finds. Evidence of Syndicate operations, displayed publicly, as art or as accusation.

The Syndicate's public relations management center in Lunacent's media district handles narrative control across all Syndicate communications platforms. Of all the facilities that Arissana Rocha Nahu could choose to exhibit, this one is the most pointed — the facility that manages what the public is told, exposed by a runner whose goal is to determine what the public is told.

The gallery does not open tonight.

TARGET: Arissana Rocha Nahu — The Lilypad Gamble
FACILITY: Syndicate Public Relations Management Center, Lunacent Media District, Narrative Control Server
OBJECTIVE: Deny exhibition-quality evidence acquisition. Protect narrative control infrastructure. Advance communications agendas.
