GHOST PROTOCOL: MAINTAINED
by Ivette Serrano-Park

Hiram 0mission ran the auxiliary servers methodically. That is the word the analyst used in the verbal debrief — methodically — and the word carries weight because methodology implies a plan formed before the engagement began, which implies intelligence, which implies that Hiram 0mission already knows more than the Syndicate is comfortable with.

What he did not find was confirmation.

Cybertrooper Pauli is a precision rig — not designed for brute force but for targeted extraction, for getting into a specific place and pulling a specific thing. The ICE the Syndicate had installed on the auxiliary servers was designed to obscure rather than stop: layers that misdirected, servers that appeared empty from the outside while the real contents nested three levels deeper than standard architecture would place them.

Hiram ran five servers. Found two agendas in one of them — not the research data, the agendas, which were there because removing them would have signaled that the server had been restructured. Two points conceded as camouflage.

The Syndicate scored seven. The research data was not accessed. Ghost protocol held.

The verbal debrief ends there. No written record. The fact of the engagement is itself under the protocol.

Hiram 0mission left with two points and no confirmation.

He will assume he missed something. He is correct.

—
Ghost protocol holds. The auxiliary servers appear empty to outside review.
He found agendas. He did not find the research.
Two points is the price of invisibility. It is acceptable.
