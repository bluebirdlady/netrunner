THE LONG GAME
by Desh Fuentes-Calloway

Loup has been patient. That is the part that concerns the threat analysis team — the patience. The party animal playbook is supposed to be impulsive, reactive, burning through viruses on reflex. But the intrusion traces on the Syndicate Archive Node show something different: deliberate reconnaissance over six operational cycles, probing edges without committing, mapping ICE patterns without triggering them.

Loup found something in the Relay Hub breach. Not data — that was contained. But a thread. A reference in a routing table to a project designation that doesn't appear in the Syndicate's public registry: Project Crow.

The Archive Node is where Project Crow's preliminary documentation lives. Feasibility studies, resource allocation projections, three years of preparatory modeling. Nothing actionable on its own — but enough to tell a runner that there is something worth finding. That there is a there there.

The ICE posture on the Archive Node has been elevated. The agenda stack includes two Crow-adjacent documents that are moving to secure escrow the moment you can advance them. Whatever Loup knows — or suspects — cannot become confirmation.

This is not about one run anymore. Loup is playing a long game. Your job is to end it short.

Keep the Project Crow documentation moving. Don't let the runner hold the thread long enough to pull it.
