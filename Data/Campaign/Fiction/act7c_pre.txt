CONTROLLED VARIABLES
by Circuito Saudade

Supervisor Andrade documented things.

It was, in his considered professional opinion, the most important thing he did. The protest movement outside the Esplanada dos Ministérios had been running for eleven months. Andrade had documented all of them: attendance figures, composition by apparent genomic phenotype, police response patterns, crowd density by quadrant and time. His facility in the Lapa Industrial Zone processed and cross-referenced data from a contract with the city's public infrastructure network. They did not conduct arrests. They did not conduct violence. They compiled information.

He was very comfortable with this distinction.

The data Andrade's facility produced had been purchased by A Teia's IP Recovery operation for nine months, supplemented with biometric pattern-matching and location inference from fourteen additional data sources. The resulting maps were extremely precise. His facility's contribution was the genomic phenotype flagging — the system that identified, in a crowd of ten thousand people, the ones who were not entirely human.

Andrade found it elegant work. Jinteki's iterative refinement process at its best: each pass of the data improving the model, the model improving the next pass. He had been with the company for eleven years. He had never seen a better demonstration of the core philosophy. Replicating Perfection. Run the experiment. Improve the variables. Run it again.

He watched the holofeed of the Esplanada protest with this thought in mind. The people outside were very loud. In forty-eight hours, he calculated, they would no longer be relevant to the outcome.

The maps his facility had compiled would be transmitted to enforcement teams the night after the vote.

It was very precise data. He was proud of it.

TARGET: Jinteki
FACILITY: Research Annex 14, Lapa Industrial Zone, São Paulo
OBJECTIVE: Extract the targeting maps before they reach enforcement. Find every location in the dataset. Get the data to the people on the list before the vote makes it a warrant.
